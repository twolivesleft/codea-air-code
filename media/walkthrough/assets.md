# Working with assets

The **Assets** section of the Codea sidebar lists Codea's asset packs.

- Click a file to open it, or drag it into a Lua file to insert its asset key.
- Drop files on a pack you can change, such as Documents, or use the add button next to it, to copy them into Codea.
- Rename an asset with F2 (Enter on Mac), and delete it with Delete (⌘⌫ on Mac).

Click the link button next to a pack to keep it in `Assets/<Pack>` in your folder, in sync like a project, so its files work with the Explorer, source control and AI coding assistants.

The Documents packs hold only their own files: their folders are your collections and projects, which you get from the Projects section.
